
# Meesho Supplier Performance Analysis
## Strategic Brief

### 1. Situation
This project analyses supplier performance over 18 months, from April 2025 to September 2026. The dashboard monitors OTIF, defect rate, average lead time, and lead-time variance.

### 2. Key Findings
- S001 achieved 88.93% OTIF.
- S002 achieved 85.94% OTIF.
- S004 achieved 88.44% OTIF.
- S003 (77.40%) and S005 (79.12%) were below the 85% OTIF target.
- S003 recorded a 7.76% defect rate, 2.98-day average lead time, and 0.96-day lead-time variance.
- S005 recorded a 6.22% defect rate.

### 3. Business Implications
Low OTIF can result in delivery delays and customer dissatisfaction. High defect rates may increase returns and replacement costs. Unpredictable lead times make inventory planning difficult.

### 4. Prioritised Recommendations

#### Priority 1: Improve At-Risk Supplier Performance
Work with S003 and S005 to identify delivery problems and set monthly OTIF improvement targets.

Expected impact: Better delivery reliability and fewer delayed orders.

#### Priority 2: Strengthen Quality Control
Introduce stricter quality checks and corrective action plans for suppliers with high defect rates.

Expected impact: Fewer defective products, returns, and replacement costs.

#### Priority 3: Improve Lead-Time Monitoring
Review lead-time trends regularly and work with suppliers to reduce delivery delays and variation.

Expected impact: Better inventory planning and more predictable deliveries.

### 5. Conclusion
The dashboard provides visibility into supplier performance and highlights areas requiring improvement. The recommendations support better delivery reliability, product quality, and supply-chain efficiency.
